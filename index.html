Inseok Kong

Hello! I am an Undergraduate Researcher in the Statistical Inference and Information Theory Lab (SIIT) at KAIST, advised by Prof. Junmo Kim. I am also an undergraduate student in the Department of GeoInformatics & AI at University of Seoul (UOS).

My research focuses on Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence with a primary focus on Computer Vision. My work spans Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) to enable vision models to adapt reliably to distribution shifts, as well as Uncertainty Calibration and object hallucination mitigation in Vision-Language Models (VLMs).
